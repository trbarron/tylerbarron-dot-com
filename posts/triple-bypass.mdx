[Triple Bypass](https://www.triplebypass.org/)

this ride is an absolute trip. Super hard, incredibly rewarding. I'm proud to cross it off my bucketlist. This helped me find my limits more than anything else has. Would recommend it to anyone seriouslythinking about it.

I came into it knowing very little about biking, exercise nutrition, FTP... any of it. In the cycling world there are many different _things_ you can dive into, study and hone. I didn’t perfect any of them, but figured I’d write down what I landed on as I tried to affordably become a cyclist

Bike stuff
* My bike: I love this thing. Worked great. I liked that it was more affordable than carbon bikes. Getting a bike with disc brakes is a must. Ended up super happy with it https://www.specialized.com/us/en/secteur-expert-disc/p/49906?color=149493-49906. Compared to what you _can_ spend a 600 dollar bike feels very affordable.  everything was stock on the bike
* [Rear Bike Light](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256804567743069.html?spm=a2g0o.order_detail.order_detail_item.3.59b1126aXfqMl2&gatewayAdapt=glo2usa): No issues. Easy four dollar spend.
* Co2 tire inflators: These worked fine but I wish I had the electronic one

Clothes & Gear

Supplements
- [High carb drink](https://blankssportsnutrition.com/products/blanks-high-carb-drink-mix?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23523748232&gbraid=0AAAABCxb4BkerGV1JvfJa6jxExzuOh8mY&gclid=Cj0KCQiA-8XXBhDaARIsADnEOrAfLY4aOdLtv4r8VBCfBD9SELteIWvjCst2FrT_WjUGddDAPffh-Z4aApBCEALw_wcB): yes. no questions. I ended up going for 70g carbs / hour and was very happy with that

- [SaltStick pills](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B002IY96B0?lv=shuf&gad_campaignid=24278545916&gclid=Cj0KCQiA-8XXBhDaARIsADnEOrCBXiD4cvAiEiZVkS6Z8ME8amxPiZ3ssE6f8bDepMpjO4aOM5YusmYaAuydEALw_wcB&maas=maas_adg_E4B09882B918689EEDDFB545C268AAAA_afap_abs&utm_campaign=24278545916&utm_medium=cpc&gbraid=0AAAAAD9RDfhnZcewWq_ZmxL-M8PZ-Ip_u&tag=maas&utm_source=google&channelId=404&ref_=aa_maas&plpRedirect=mhFallback&th=1): yes. no questions.

Misc
* MyVeloFit bike fitting: yes. Worth the time, worth the money. It helped in little ways that paid off a ton https://www.myvelofit.com/
* Bike tune up: Z’s bikes on Colfax helped me out. I felt a lot more comfortable knowing someone else had looked over the bike. Lots of people try to get their bike tuned the month of the Triple. Be smart about that
* Podcasts: I got into Some Work, All Play for a bit. Helped a lot with the nutrition and general physiology discussion https://swaprunning.com/
* Youtube: I did watch a lot of GCN. I don't think its really worth the time but it was fun https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCuTaETsuCOkJ0H_GAztWt0Q
* Claude: Asking AI about general nutrition, pacing, metrics, etc was helpful. I had it make me a nutrition plan which was a good call.
