The [Triple Bypass](https://www.triplebypass.org/) is an absolute trip. Super hard, incredibly rewarding, and something I’m proud to cross off my bucket list. It helped me find my limits more than anything else has. If you’re seriously thinking about it, I’d recommend it.

I came into it knowing very little about biking, exercise nutrition, FTP—any of it. In cycling, there are a lot of different things you can dive into, study, and hone. I didn’t perfect any of them, but I figured I’d write down what I landed on while trying to affordably become a cyclist.

These are my notes on what I used, what felt worth the money, and what I’d skip.

## Bike

- **[Specialized Secteur Expert Disc](https://www.specialized.com/us/en/secteur-expert-disc/p/49906?color=149493-49906).** I love this thing. It worked great, and I liked that it was more affordable than the carbon bikes I was looking at. Compared with what you *can* spend, a $600 bike felt very affordable. Everything was stock. Disc brakes were a must for me, and I ended up super happy with it.
- **[Rear light](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256804567743069.html).** No issues. An easy four-dollar spend.
- **CO₂ tire inflators.** These worked fine, but I wish I’d gotten an electronic inflator.

## Clothes and gear

- **[Gloves](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256808491452169.html).** Nice to have. I wish I’d gotten them earlier.
- **[Specialized Align II helmet](https://www.specialized.com/us/en/align-ii/p/1000207992?color=1000207996-1000207992).** This was the internet’s go-to suggestion.
- **Bibs and shorts.** Mostly [this AliExpress brand](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256811703003647.html), plus a Rapha set I splurged on. I ended up collecting a few pairs of shorts and bibs. I preferred bibs for rides longer than an hour. The Rapha set was worth the money; it’s amazing how good it is.
- **[Socks](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256809816699745.html).** People online go crazy about aero socks, so I paid for some. They’re nice!
- **[Shoes](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BHFFP9X4).** These were about $20 when I got them. They worked great.
- **[Sunglasses](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256807958425146.html).** I tried a few pairs. I have a big face, and I ended up happy with these. I wish [these Falcon-looking ones](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256812287655490.html) had worked better, though.
- **Water bottles.** I got [one insulated](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07HGTFKHD) and [one non-insulated](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07HGR3S45). Insulation didn’t matter for what I was doing. I wish both had been non-insulated.
- **Water pack.** I already had one from running. I love water, and I was really glad I brought it. It was a lifesaver.
- **[OpenSwim headphones](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CS3JBP67).** I was too scared to wear these on the ride, but they’re great for running. More people used headphones during the ride than I expected.
- **Chest heart-rate monitor.** I got a Peloton-branded one on Facebook Marketplace for $10. It worked fine, and having heart-rate zones was useful. Worth the money.

## Fuel and supplements

- **[High-carb drink mix](https://blankssportsnutrition.com/products/blanks-high-carb-drink-mix).** Yes. No questions. I ended up aiming for 70 grams of carbs per hour and was very happy with that.
- **[Hydrogel mix](https://blankssportsnutrition.com/products/hydrogel-mix).** Not very important for me. I had it, but I never felt like it did anything. It would be the first thing I’d cut.
- **[Sodium bicarb](https://blankssportsnutrition.com/products/bicarb-mix).** Beyond unnecessary for me. I’d skip it, but if you listen to enough podcasts, you get curious.
- **[SaltStick pills](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B002IY96B0).** Yes. No questions. I was glad to have them.
- **Honey Stinger gels.** I tried making my own gels. Those sucked. These were good, and the caffeinated options let me get some caffeine in while eating them.

## Consumables

- **Roll-on sunscreen.** Good to have mid-ride. I liked it more than spray sunscreen.
- **[Chamois Butt’r](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00D535IAG).** Yes. No questions. I’d get a big one and some little ones. This was absolutely needed for my long rides.
- **[Body soap](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0009EILAS).** A softer soap was helpful for keeping high-friction skin healthy.

## Indoor training

- **Smart trainer.** I got a Saris H2 on Facebook Marketplace. It works fine. I wish it had virtual shifting and were quieter.
- **[Zwift](https://www.zwift.com/).** I found this super fun and rewarding. I had a weird schedule with a newborn, and I liked watching YouTube videos while riding, so it fit really well into my life.
- **[Alpe d’Huez poster](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256808014489106.html).** Borderline must-have.
- **[Big fan](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B077BMBR5B).** You think I’m joking, but this was a must-have.

## Fitting and training resources

- **[MyVeloFit](https://www.myvelofit.com/).** Worth the time and money. The bike fitting helped in little ways that paid off a ton.
- **Bike tune-up.** Z’s Bikes on Colfax helped me out. I felt a lot more comfortable knowing someone else had looked over the bike. Lots of people try to get their bikes tuned the month of the Triple, so I’d plan ahead.
- **[Some Work, All Play](https://swaprunning.com/).** I got into this podcast for a bit. It helped a lot with the nutrition and general physiology discussion.
- **[GCN on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCuTaETsuCOkJ0H_GAztWt0Q).** I watched a lot of it. I don’t think it was really worth the time, but it was fun.
- **Claude.** Asking AI about general nutrition, pacing, and metrics was helpful. I had it make me a nutrition plan, which was a good call for me.
